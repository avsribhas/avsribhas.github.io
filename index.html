§1Premise

The only confirmed instance of general intelligence runs on biological substrates that process information electrically. I predict those network dynamics, extract the computational principles, and use them to design AI architectures that current approaches are missing. I also work on alignment, where my position is that finite constraints cannot cover an infinite action space, and the alternative is engineering what the system can perceive.
